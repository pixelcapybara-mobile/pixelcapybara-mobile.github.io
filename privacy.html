KotobaLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

KotobaLens ("the app") is an app for reading and learning Japanese. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Who we are

The app is published by PixelCapybara. Contact: pixelcapybara.mobile@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your pages, scanned images, sentences, saved words, flashcards, review history, folders, glossary and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, we do not have accounts, and we cannot see or access this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. If you make a backup in Settings, it is saved only where you choose to save it.

What is sent off your device

The app has no servers of its own. It connects only to Google, for three things: the AI features (Gemini), ads (AdMob) and purchases (Google Play).

AI features (Google Gemini)

When you use the following features, the app sends data directly from your device to Google's Gemini API, using an API key that you create and enter yourself:

  • Scanning: the page image you scan or import is sent to Gemini to recognise its text.
  • Translation, "Explain in context", sentence breakdown, grammar practice and "Fix words": the Japanese sentence (and, for translation, names from your glossary) is sent to Gemini.

This data is handled by Google under its own terms and privacy policy for the Gemini API (Gemini API terms, Google Privacy Policy). Depending on whether you use a free or paid key, Google may use submitted content to improve its products; check the terms that apply to your key. We do not receive a copy.

If Gemini is unavailable, the app can fall back to on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit), which does not send the image anywhere.

The dictionary and the Japanese word splitter run entirely on your device.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google's ad software may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address,
  • information about your device (model, operating system, language),
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps), and
  • diagnostic information about ad loading.

This data goes directly from your device to Google and is handled under Google's policies (How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy). We do not receive your advertising ID or any personal information from Google; we only see totals such as how many ads were shown. Your pages, sentences, words and flashcards are never shared with the ad software.

Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own the purchase so that it can turn ads off, including after you reinstall the app.

Advertising ID and your consent choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your consent, using Google's consent form, before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Privacy choices (shown where this applies).

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads). If you do, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Buying "Remove ads" turns ads off completely: the banner is no longer shown and the ad software is not started.

Your API key

Your Gemini API key is stored encrypted on your device (Android Keystore) and is sent only to Google to authorise your requests. We never see it. It is not shared with the ad software and is not included in backups.

What we do not do

We do not run analytics or crash reporting of our own. We do not sell your data. Apart from Google, as described above (for Gemini, ads and purchases), no one receives data from the app.

Permissions

The app uses the internet to reach the Gemini API, to show ads and to check purchases with Google Play. Google's ad software uses the advertising ID permission. Purchases use Google Play's billing permission. Photos are taken with your device's camera app or chosen from your gallery or files only when you ask to scan; the app does not otherwise access your camera, photos or files.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • You can use the app without entering an API key. Your pages and sentences are then not sent anywhere. Ads, unless removed, still connect to Google as described above.
  • You can manage ad consent in Settings → Ads → Privacy choices (where shown), and your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • You can turn ads off by buying "Remove ads".
  • You can delete stored page images, cached translations, cards and everything else in the app's settings, or by uninstalling.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and the new policy will apply from then.
