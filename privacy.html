KotobaLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

KotobaLens ("the app") is an app for reading and learning Japanese. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Who we are

The app is published by PixelCapybara. Contact: pixelcapybara.mobile@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your pages, scanned images, sentences, saved words, flashcards, review history, folders, glossary and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, we do not have accounts, and we cannot see or access this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

What is sent off your device

The app has no servers of its own. When you use the following features, the app sends data directly from your device to Google's Gemini API, using an API key that you create and enter yourself:

  • Scanning: the page image you scan or import is sent to Gemini to recognise its text.
  • Translation, "Explain in context", sentence breakdown and "Fix words": the Japanese sentence (and, for translation, names from your glossary) is sent to Gemini.

This data is handled by Google under its own terms and privacy policy for the Gemini API (Gemini API terms, Google Privacy Policy). Depending on whether you use a free or paid key, Google may use submitted content to improve its products; check the terms that apply to your key. We do not receive a copy.

If Gemini is unavailable, the app can fall back to on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit), which does not send the image anywhere.

The dictionary and the Japanese word splitter run entirely on your device.

Your API key

Your Gemini API key is stored encrypted on your device (Android Keystore) and is sent only to Google to authorise your requests. We never see it.

What we do not do

No ads. No analytics. No crash reporting. No tracking. No selling or sharing of data with anyone other than Google as described above, and only when you use those features.

Permissions

The app uses the internet to reach the Gemini API. Photos are taken with your device's camera app or chosen from your gallery or files only when you ask to scan; the app does not otherwise access your camera, photos or files.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You can use the app without entering a key, in which case no data is sent anywhere. You can delete stored page images, cached translations, cards and everything else in the app's settings, or by uninstalling.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and the new policy will apply from then.
